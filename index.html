How to play

Classic 75-ball bingo against the computer. Complete a row, column, or diagonal first.

  1. Each card has five columns: B, I, N, G, O. The centre square is free.
  2. A ball is called every few seconds, depending on the difficulty.
  3. Mark called numbers on your card. Earlier calls stay valid.
  4. The computer marks its own card 1.7 seconds after each call.
  5. A line counts when the round ends. Both in the same round is a draw.
  6. Three wrong marks end the game.
